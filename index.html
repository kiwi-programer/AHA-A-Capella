Meet Poco, the Piano Penguin 🐧

Poco once had an idea — a spark of passion to bring voices together at school. One by one, they gathered singers, building a group from nothing but a dream. With vision and heart, Poco led that choir forward, and what began as an idea blossomed into AHA!: a talented, tight-knit group of singers who perform with harmony and purpose. Poco is our mascot, our origin story, and our reminder that great things start with one voice.
